# Creative Agency Brief Form

A structured client brief intake form with multi-select service cards, segmented investment budget selectors, timeline pills, and clear requirements.

## Features
- **Capability Cards**: High-contrast selectable checkboxes for brand, product design, design systems, and engineering.
- **Budget & Timeline Selectors**: Accessible styled radio pills with `:focus-visible` focus rings.
- **Responsive Stacking**: Seamlessly shifts from a 2-column agency card layout to single-column mobile view.
- **Demo Validation**: Form validates all required inputs and outputs an honest client brief confirmation.

## Tokens
- Prefix: `.cf-agency-*`
- Background: `#f5f4ef`
- Surface: `#ffffff`
- Ink: `#111111`
- Border: `#dedbd2`
