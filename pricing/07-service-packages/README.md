# 07 Product / Service Packages Pricing

A professional service offering presenting three defined engineering and design packages (Design System Sprint, Full Platform Overhaul, Architecture Retainer) with concrete scopes, delivery timelines, and milestone deliverables rather than generic SaaS features.

## Design Highlights
- **Tangible deliverables & timelines**: Explicit scopes (2-Week Intensive, 6-Week Turnkey, Ongoing Retainer) and concrete handoff items (tokens, component repos, PR integration).
- **Interactive Statement of Work (SOW) modal**: Prospective clients can inspect exact weekly milestone schedules and delivery criteria via a modal dialog with Escape/overlay dismiss.
- **Warm studio aesthetic**: Earthy stone tones (`#fafaf9`), warm bronze highlights (`#d97706`), and clean typography.
- **Target suitability indicators**: "Ideal For" callouts clarify who each engagement model is tailored to serve.

## Class Prefix
All styles are strictly scoped under `.pr-pack-*`.
