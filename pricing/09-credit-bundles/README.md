# 09 Credit / Token Bundles Pricing

A modern fintech prepaid token and compute credit wallet featuring an active tenant balance bar, 4 tiered coin-stack cards (Micro, Builder, Production, Enterprise Scale) with transparent unit economics, and an interactive workload requirement estimator with dynamic bundle matching.

## Design Highlights
- **Fintech & Token Wallet Aesthetic**: Warm cream/ivory canvas (`#faf8f5`), crisp white cards (`#ffffff`), royal violet (`#7c3aed`), amber coin accents (`#f59e0b`), and mint green (`#10b981`).
- **Live Tenant Wallet Bar**: Displays simulated active tenant balance (`12,450 Credits`), team wallet ID, and perpetual rollover guarantees.
- **Transparent Unit Economics**: Shows per-credit rates ($0.050 down to $0.018/credit) with truthful percentage savings badges (Save 20%, 40%, 64%) and custom SVG coin stack graphics.
- **Interactive Workload Estimator**: Workload type selector and volume slider dynamically calculate required credits and automatically highlight the optimal recommended pack card.
- **No Artificial Scarcity**: Perpetual credit validity with zero monthly lock-in or recurring fees.

## Class Prefix
All styles are strictly scoped under `.pr-bundle-*`.
