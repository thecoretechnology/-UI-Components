# 10 Contact-for-Pricing / Enterprise Governance

An executive institutional agreement and bespoke architecture suite articulating compliance accreditations, 3 core institutional pillars, and an interactive scope intake generator for enterprise procurement without fabricated customer testimonials or vanity logos.

## Design Highlights
- **Executive White-Glove Aesthetic**: Warm platinum ivory canvas (`#fbfbfa` / `#f8f8f6`), deep obsidian typography (`#09090b`), refined corporate borders (`#e4e4e7`), and deep sapphire accents (`#1e3a8a`).
- **Accreditation Seal Bar**: Displays 5 institutional compliance shields (SOC 2 Type II, 99.995% Uptime SLA Remedy, HIPAA BAA Executable, ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS, GDPR & Sovereign In-Region).
- **3 Pillars of Governance**: Replaces plain lists with 3 distinct architectural pillar cards (Dedicated Sovereign Isolation, Custom Legal & Procurement, Executive White-Glove Support).
- **Formal Scope Intake & RFP Generator Workbench**: Collects legal organization entity, executive email, deployment architecture, projected event throughput, and compliance requirements, generating an instant demo tracking reference token (e.g., `MSA-8491`).
- **Mutual NDA & Strict Privacy**: Inquiries governed by mutual non-disclosure expectations with 48-hour diligence guarantee.

## Class Prefix
All styles are strictly scoped under `.pr-ent-*`.
