# Modular Tectonic Arrangement Hero (Atelier Tectonique)

An architectural hero layout based on unequal, interlocking monolithic slabs and expressed structural expansion seams (tectonics), rejecting cookie-cutter card grids in favor of massive spatial compression.

## Design Movement & Rationale
- **Movement**: Tectonic Earth Architecture & Mass Timber Formwork (Zürich / Geneva).
- **Asymmetric Grid**: Constructed from five unequal volumetric blocks separated by 4px black expansion seams, conveying tectonic weight and structural honesty.
- **Ecological Engineering Rigor**: Focuses on authentic material lifecycles, embodied carbon (-48 kg CO₂e/m² for timber), and demountable compression joints.
- **Zero Remote Image Dependencies**: All textures, isometric mortise-and-tenon diagrams, and slabs are generated purely via CSS gradients and inline SVGs.

## Interactive Features
- **Tectonic Material Switcher**: Dynamically swap between European Spruce CLT, Unstabilized Rammed Earth, and Split Alpine Basalt to inspect surface finishes and live carbon sequestration metrics.
- **Joint Vector Diagram**: Technical 1:5 scale structural detail showing dry-fit tenon stress points.
- **Audit Verification Action**: Dispatches verified Environmental Product Declaration telemetry.
