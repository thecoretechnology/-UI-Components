# Playful Graphic Constructivist Hero (Bauhaus Form Lab)

An intentional, dynamic hero section built on the foundational geometric vocabulary of Bauhaus Dessau (Kandinsky, Klee, Moholy-Nagy). Replaces generic colorful squiggles with disciplined primary geometry, unexpected scale, and kinetic tension.

## Design Movement & Rationale
- **Movement**: Bauhaus Constructivism & Elementary Form Pedagogy (Dessau, 1926).
- **Elemental Shapes**: Circles with cutouts, semicircles, triangles, and monolithic pillars interacting with deliberate asymmetry.
- **Strict Constructivist Triad**: Vermilion Red (`#ef4444`), Cobalt Blue (`#1d4ed8`), and Chrome Canary Yellow (`#facc15`) anchored by pure Black (`#111111`) and milk white.
- **Zero Remote Image Dependencies**: All geometric shapes and compositions are generated purely with CSS borders, border-radii, and clip-paths.

## Interactive Features
- **Kinetic Harmony Switcher**: Transition the visual sculpture between Equilibrium, Diagonal Tension, and Centrifugal Harmony modes.
- **Master Rotate Forms**: Interactively spin the geometric assembly in alternating directions.
- **Tactile Shape Clicks**: Click individual geometric elements directly to trigger spring rotations and scale feedback.
