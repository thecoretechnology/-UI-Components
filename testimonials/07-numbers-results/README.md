# 07 Numbers and Results Testimonial

A results-driven case study testimonial anchored by 3 primary quantified business and engineering metrics with an interactive deep-dive ledger and explicit demonstration disclaimers.

## Design Highlights
- **Impactful Numeric Typography**: 3 prominent metric cards highlighting uptime (99.999%), velocity (4.2x), and cost (-64%).
- **Interactive Deep-Dive Ledger**: Selecting any metric dynamically updates the architectural breakdown ledger.
- **Honest Fictional Labeling**: Prominently displays demonstration and simulation badges to ensure no real endorsement is implied.
- **Keyboard Friendly**: Metric cards are accessible as toggle buttons with `role="button"` and `aria-pressed`.

## Class Prefix
All styles are strictly scoped under `.ts-metrics-*`.
