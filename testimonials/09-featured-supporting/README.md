# 09 Featured Testimonial with Supporting Quotes

An asymmetric 60/40 composition showcasing a dominant primary enterprise spotlight review flanked by a vertical stack of smaller supporting peer reviews from engineering and compliance perspectives.

## Design Highlights
- **Clear Visual Hierarchy**: The featured card commands 60% of horizontal space with prominent metrics, an elevated review ribbon, and detailed enterprise SLA tags.
- **Supporting Peer Context**: Two stacked secondary cards provide succinct complementary verification from compliance and tech lead stakeholders.
- **Warm Stone Palette**: Modern, sophisticated warm stone background (`#fafaf9`) with high-contrast text (`#1c1917`) and amber accents (`#d97706`).
